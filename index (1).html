Quick Reminders
💪
Sharp Motions
Straight arms, strong wrists, shoulders down. Hit every motion on the count.
📢
Use Your Voice
Motions mean nothing without volume. Project from your diaphragm, not your throat.
🦵
Land Safe
Soft knees on every jump landing. Never lock out. Both feet touch at the same time.
👀
Eyes Up
Look at the crowd, not the ground. Confidence shows. Smile — you're performing.
